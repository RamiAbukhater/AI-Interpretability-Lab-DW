Introduce yourself

Before we get into models and activations, let's get to know each other. Write a short, discussion-post-style introduction and share it with the group on Discord.

Due: Friday, October 23, by the end of the day

What to do

Post a short introduction in the general research chat on our Discord. A few sentences is plenty; write it the way you would a discussion post, in your own voice. Include:

  1. Your name
  2. Your year
  3. Your major
  4. Why you joined DataWorks research
  5. Your favorite spot on the UCSD campus

A template, if you want one

Feel free to write it however you like. If you would rather fill in the blanks, start from this:

Hi everyone! I'm [name], a [year] studying [major].
I joined DataWorks research because [your reason].
My favorite spot on campus is [spot], because [why you like it].

How to submit

The Discord post is the submission. There is nothing else to upload. Post it in the general research chat by the end of the day on Friday, October 23.

Not in the Discord yet? Ask Rami or Akshar for an invite.

While you are there

Read a few of your labmates' posts. You will be choosing teams for Homework 2 later in the quarter, so it helps to know who is interested in what. Then start on Setup on Kaggle, which has a couple of steps that involve waiting.
